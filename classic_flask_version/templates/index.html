Project Abstract & Problem Statement

This project implements an unsupervised machine learning system for automated customer segmentation. Traditional manual customer classification fails to capture complex, multi-dimensional purchasing behaviors. By leveraging distance-based and density-based clustering algorithms, this system partitions customer records into distinct demographic and purchasing cohorts without needing manual ground-truth labels.

Frequently Asked Viva Questions & Answers

Because customer datasets do not come with ground-truth target labels. The goal is to discover natural mathematical structure and grouping inherent in feature space without human supervision.

1. Initialize K cluster centroids.
2. Assign each data point to its nearest centroid based on Euclidean distance.
3. Recalculate centroids as the mean of assigned points.
4. Repeat until centroids converge.

The Elbow Method plots Within-Cluster Sum of Squares (WCSS) against cluster count K. The point where WCSS reduction sharply slows down (forming an 'elbow') represents the optimal trade-off between compactness and cluster complexity.

Distance-based algorithms (K-Means, DBSCAN) rely on Euclidean distance. If Income is in $100,000s and Age is in 10s, income would dominate distance calculations. StandardScaler rescales features to mean=0 and std=1.
